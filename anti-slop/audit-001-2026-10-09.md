# PitchTicker visual audit — 9 October 2026

Playwright inspected the local fixture preview at http://127.0.0.1:8099.
Home, fixtures, fixture detail, news and admin were rendered at 390px and 1440px in light and dark themes: 20 combinations, no horizontal document overflow.
Screenshots visually inspected: desktop home, full mobile home, desktop admin light and dark. These are test fixtures, not production content; sparse rows, repeated headlines and absent imagery limit judgments about content density.

## Findings

1. MEDIUM — R-05, C-3: Home repeats the same heading/filter/league card/footer structure for today's fixtures and live fixtures. The duplication gives two sections equal weight and consumes space before useful scores. Consolidate the match board with an actionable live filter and remove repeated league footer text already present in the group heading.
2. MEDIUM — R-20, C-3: Mobile puts league navigation above today's matches. The first match starts at y=492px with the 46px preview notice present. Move league discovery behind a compact control so scores lead the page; do not count the fixture-only notice as production UI.
3. MEDIUM — R-05, R-20: Admin gives a single news-refresh action a panel as tall as the five scraper actions. The large empty panel comes from equal-height columns rather than the amount of work in each section. Use a compact news action and prioritize running/failed jobs and scraper controls.
4. LOW — C-1, R-05: Repeated rounded borders, similar surface colors and modest type differences make home and admin feel like generic card layouts. Reserve containers for meaningful groups, strengthen match/date hierarchy and use row separators for dense data.
5. LOW — C-4: Fixture detail renders no h1 in both themes and widths. Give the fixture an accessible descriptive page heading without adding an oversized hero.
